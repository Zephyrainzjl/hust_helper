## 中文

### 项目来源与致谢

`hust_helper` 的产生来自三类长期积累：

1. 作者所在材料学院的课题组在华中科技大学多年的学习、科研和共同吃饭经历；
2. 华中科技大学机械学院某位师兄编写并提供的《HUSTer 的干饭修养——HUST 求学七年的干饭经验》PDF，原 PDF 署名为“许少 著”；
3. 作者每一位朋友在华科校园和武汉各处形成的真实饮食体验、推荐、补充和修订。

这些来源共同构成了 `hust_eater` 初始数据与后续扩展的重要基础。

## English

### Project origin and acknowledgements

`hust_helper` was created from three long-term sources of experience:

1. years of study, research, and shared meals within the author's research group at Huazhong University of Science and Technology;
2. the PDF guide *HUSTer 的干饭修养——HUST 求学七年的干饭经验*, written and provided by a senior fellow from the HUST School of Mechanical Science and Engineering and credited in the source to “许少”; and
3. real dining experiences, recommendations, additions, and corrections shared by every friend of the author across the HUST campus and Wuhan.

Together, these sources form the foundation of the initial `hust_eater` dataset and its continuing expansion.
